# Power BI – Data Transformation and Reporting

This project focuses on preparing and organizing business data for reporting in Power BI. I worked through the data preparation process, explored how the structure of the data affects analysis, and built a report to make key information easier to understand.

## Project Objectives

- Prepare the source data for reporting and analysis.
- Improve the structure and consistency of the data.
- Explore how data preparation supports more reliable business reporting.
- Build Power BI visuals to present key metrics and trends.

## Deliverables

- **Prepared dataset:** Data organized for analysis and reporting.
- **Power BI report:** Visuals to explore business metrics and trends.
- **PBIX project file:** The completed exercise, including the data preparation and report.

**Tools:** Power BI Desktop, Data Transformation, Data Visualization, Business Reporting
