# Power BI – Sales and Profitability Dashboard

A Power BI project focused on exploring sales performance and profitability through business reporting and data visualization. The dashboard brings key performance indicators and sales metrics together to support analysis of business performance.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze sales and profitability to understand overall business performance.
- Identify trends and differences in sales and profit across relevant business dimensions.
- Use Power BI visuals to make performance metrics easier to interpret.
- Explore how interactive reporting can support business analysis and decision-making.

## Deliverables

- **Power BI dashboard:** Visualizations for reviewing sales performance, profitability, and relevant business trends.
- **Business performance analysis:** Exploration of sales and profit metrics to identify patterns and areas for further investigation.
- **PBIX project file:** The completed Power BI report, including the data model, calculations, and dashboard.

**Tools:** Power BI Desktop, Data Modeling, Data Visualization, Business Intelligence
